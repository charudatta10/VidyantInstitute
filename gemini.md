### 🔁 **Academic Progression Framework (4+1+2+1+2 years)**

| Stage        | Duration | Credential | Focus |
|--------------|----------|------------|-------|
| 🧱 Foundation | 4 years  | Foundational Certificate | Cognitive scaffolding, PKM, interdisciplinary exposure |
| 🎓 Diploma    | 1 year   | Digital Diploma NFT | Applied skills, domain-specific orientation |
| 🎓 Degree     | 2 years  | Bachelor's NFT | Research basics, modular inquiry, community projects |
| 🎓 Masters    | 1 year   | Master's NFT | Advanced synthesis, AI-assisted research |
| 🎓 PhD        | 2 years  | Doctoral NFT | Original contribution, DAO governance, open publication |

---

### 🌐 **Core Principles**

- **DAO Governance**: Learner-led decision-making, curriculum voting, peer credentialing
- **Remote & Distributed**: Global access, asynchronous modules, decentralized instructors
- **AI-Powered Learning**: Adaptive syllabus, personalized feedback, multimodal delivery
- **NFT Credentials**: Immutable, verifiable degrees with embedded portfolios and remix rights

---

### 🧠 **Adaptive Learning Engine**

- **Granular Evaluation**: Daily micro-assessments, cumulative tracking
- **Syllabus Evolution**: Real-time updates based on learner performance and feedback
- **Learning Styles**:
  - 🎨 *Visual*: Comics, diagrams, reels
  - 🔍 *Root*: Deep theory, lineage tracing
  - 🎧 *Listener*: Podcasts, voice notes, AI narration
  - 🕹️ *Tactile*: Interactive simulations, virtual labs
